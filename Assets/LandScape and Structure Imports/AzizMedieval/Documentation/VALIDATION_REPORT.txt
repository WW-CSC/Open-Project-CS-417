DOORWAY MASONRY

include matching arched reveals. The original stone meshes, UVs and arch pivots

arch joints, jambs and wall reveals; 69 checks confirmed the opening stays clear.
Six close views were reviewed in Blender and six in Unity, covering each
entrance from inside and outside. The five physical routes were rerun after
the correction and passed. This check covers the repaired door surrounds.
